## What this does not claim

- **A generic ETB/bundle/display is not 1:1.** The catalogue applies one pack count and one layout to the whole kind. The manifest marks those counts `modelled` rather than copying the set’s booster odds and calling it product-specific.
- **A synthesised ETB promo is not the real promo.** The field is `unverified` with `value: null`; the opener’s deterministic pick remains a game mechanic, not a sourced box list.
- **30th Celebration is a ten-card abstraction.** The physical wrapper is six cards. The game stretches it to ten, and the manifest says so instead of presenting the deal as the wrapper.
- **The 30C UPC’s three-card Classic pack is not opened.** The pack count and physical total include it; the layout records it under `omitted`.
- **Hit tiers are the era model unless a set has its own sample.** The source line in each field says which kind of claim it is. Estimated eras are `modelled`.
- **No product photo or wrapper checklist was used for a generic product.** A per-product source is what would promote its fields from `modelled` to `verified`.
