## What is painted, and what that claim is

`public/foil/tinsel/sheet.png` is a **procedural reconstruction**: 64
horizontal lines at a 16px pitch, 236 breaks,
43.17% coverage, peak 1.000. It is drawn from
Bulbapedia's sentence, not copied from a card. No scan or angled photograph of a
Tinsel sheet exists in this repository, so `textureVerified` is false in the
generated index and every field built from it.

The stripes **tile** (measured seam 0.11%), and the pitch is this
build's own choice: the source names the pattern's orientation, not a line count
per card or a repeat. Both are disclosed, not presented as fact.

## Not painted: Black Bolt & White Flare

Bulbapedia Rarity: "Tinsel Holofoil returned as a once-off in Black Bolt and White Flare, with holofoil rare cards in these sets also having Tinsel Holofoil borders."

The Black Bolt & White Flare set page marks Tinsel Holofoil on three cards only — 001, 011 and 021 — each a Black Bolt & White Flare Poster Collection exclusive, and TCGdex registers exactly those three variants as tinsel. Those are promotional printings this catalogue does not carry.

Neither set is painted: the sources disagree on whether every holo rare or only three promos carry it, and the sentence that does cover the holo rares describes a BORDER, which is a different foil area from the striped image reproduced here.

## Promotional printings

Bulbapedia says Tinsel was also used "along with various promotional cards", but names none of them, and this catalogue does not resolve promos by set. No promotional printing makes a Tinsel claim here.

## Validation

`tmp/check-tinsel.ts` proves, per set: every holo rare resolves to Tinsel with the
art window, a common and a Rare Holo EX make no claim, a reverse printing of a holo
rare keeps its reverse paper, the sheet's lines are horizontal and tile, a card's
cut is stable between frames, and the render is the scan at rest and adds light only
inside the mask at tilt.

It does **not** compare the sheet to a photograph, because none is in this repo.
