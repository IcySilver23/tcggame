## What is painted, and what that claim is

`public/foil/mirror/sheet.png` is a **procedural reconstruction**: a uniform mirror
modulated along one diagonal, 3 bands, coverage 100.00%,
floor 0.551, peak 0.949. The paper prints no marks, so
there is no dot, star or stripe to draw: the sheen is light, and the mask still
decides where foil exists.

It **tiles** (measured seam 0.19%). The `contrast`, the number
of bands and the treatment of an angle-dependent glare as a fixed anisotropic ramp
are this build's own choices, disclosed rather than presented as measured facts. No
scan or angled photograph of the sheet exists in this repository, so
`textureVerified` is false in the generated index and every field built from it.

## One paper, two names

Bulbapedia, Mega Evolution set page: the set "reintroduces Mirror Holofoil variants of cards, last seen during the Black & White Series".

Bulbapedia, Holofoil page: the same finish is described as "Sheen Holofoil … used for all Holo cards from XY, and on various promotional cards".

This build treats Mirror and Sheen as one paper with one texture, named `mirror`. A printing that the sources call either resolves here; the side of the name a card carries is recorded in its own row, not in the renderer.

## The language axis

The direction is a language axis, not a per-card one: Japanese cards run bottom left to top right across the whole face, Korean the same over the image only, and international (English) cards bottom right to top left over the image. This catalogue holds English printings, so that is the direction modelled; a Japanese printing would need its own texture, and none is claimed here.

## Neighbours

The Crosshatch promos have their own lattice sheet (crosshatch-index.ts) and are not painted as Mirror. The textured BW promos wear an embossed paper with no marks at all, so they are painted as the BW relief (reports/textured-printings.md) rather than borrowing this one. The row no source lists makes no claim at all.

## Validation

`tmp/check-mirror.ts` proves: the sheet is a smooth diagonal band that never goes
dark and tiles; the field is uniform foil varying along the direction the source
names; every one of the nine claims resolves to Mirror with the token it needs and no
invented reverse printing; the neighbouring treatments keep their own papers; a card
at rest is the scan byte for byte and adds light only inside its mask at tilt; and a
mirror never renders as an unpatterned plain field.

It does **not** compare the sheet to a photograph, because none is in this repo.
