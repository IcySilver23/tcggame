## Method

Modern means a set release date on or after 2023-01-01. The evidence table names the physical pack count and any sealed promo; the manifest is rebuilt from the same database the shop reads. A missing row, a pack-count disagreement, or a promo-name disagreement is a failure in `tmp/check-sealed-evidence.ts`. Products that never existed are removed from `pack_products`, not hidden: every remaining row is either one the shop can stock or one of the three pull-table hosts listed above, and the ids in the table above are what stops them being generated again.
