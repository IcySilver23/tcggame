## Where the paper sits

The source says the crosshatch effect is "often applied to the background of the card, as in a Reverse Holo card", so these rows foil the body and text box with the illustration window left matte. "Often" is not "always" and no photograph is in this repo, so maskVerified stays false.

## What is painted, and what that claim is

`public/foil/crosshatch/sheet.png` is a **procedural reconstruction**: two families
of thin diagonal lines at 64px pitch, 16 lines each, crossing
into diamonds — coverage 45.89%, peak 1.000, and a
deliberately dark 0.025 mean field between the lines, which is what makes a
line read as a line. The pitch, the line width, the per-line intensity and the
breaks are this build's drawings: the source names the two families and the grid,
not a count or a repeat. No scan or angled photograph of a Crosshatch card exists in
this repository, so `textureVerified` is false in the generated index and every
field built from it.

The sheet **tiles**: the lattice repeats every 64px and 16 cells fit
the sheet exactly, so the line at the last column continues at the first. The measured
wrap step is 2.52%, which is one pixel of the lattice's own
gradient rather than a phase jump, so a card may sample any window.

## When the treatment stopped

Bulbapedia Holofoil: the pattern is "generally used only for cards awarded at Play! Pokémon events" — which is what the three BW Victory Cup promos are.

Bulbapedia, Miscellaneous Promotional cards: "The Crosshatch Holofoil treatment was no longer used on cards starting with the 2015-2016 season and was replaced by the Mirror Holofoil treatment (on cards from BREAKthrough only) followed by the standard Parallel Holofoil treatment."

No later Play! Pokémon award is painted by habit: this build paints the three cards its printing table names Crosshatch and nothing else, because "an award card" is not evidence of a paper.

## Neighbours

The textured BW promos wear no mark sheet at all: their paper is embossed, so they are painted as the BW relief (reports/textured-printings.md) and this lattice is never lent to them. The row no source lists makes no claim at all.

## Validation

`tmp/check-crosshatch.ts` proves: the sheet carries two line families that cross into
a grid, a dark field between them, determinism, and the measured repeat; the sampler
wraps instead of clamping at the edge; all three claims resolve to Crosshatch
with the body mask; the neighbouring papers keep theirs; a card at rest is the scan
byte for byte, adds light only inside its mask at tilt, and a line over printed white
adds nothing.

It does **not** compare the sheet to a photograph, because none is in this repo.
