## What is verified and what is drawn

- Bulbapedia describes nested tiles with type symbols of different sizes. That identifies the paper, not its exact geometry or artwork.
- The nested lattice and glyphs are procedural drawings made for this build, not official icons, and none is verified against a photograph.
- The sheet tiles; whether the real print repeated exactly is undocumented, so tiling is this build's disclosed choice.
- The type comes from the card; an unknown type renders plain foil rather than the wrong symbol.
- Of 2139 reverse printings here, 2130 have a usable type; the rest show plain foil.
