## Notes

- **me6** (sv): Legendary Stadium halves are dealt after the pools and swapped apart, so this set is checked for pool membership rather than slot order, and its reverse count allows for the pair landing on a reverse slot.

## Not swept

Subset-only sets make no sealed product of their own, so their leftover tables are not judged as packs: me55c. Their cards stay in the catalogue; the shelf and the pack-battle picker do not offer them.

## Method

Each set is dealt 200 packs. A wrong card count, a rare-or-hit count outside the plan's slots (plus the foil rare a subset miss deals where the pack prints no reverse), a hit rate outside 3σ, a reverse or Basic Energy that should not be there, a card out of its slot or a wrong finish is an outlier. A host subset's miss rate is measured where the subset card sits on the reverse position, and a Legendary Stadium half is allowed in any non-hit position because it is dealt outside the pools. With 132 sets, about 0.4 three-sigma flags per sweep are expected from chance alone, so a flagged set is re-drawn at a larger N before it is believed.
