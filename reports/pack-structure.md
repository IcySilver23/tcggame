## Not swept

Subset-only sets make no sealed product of their own, so their leftover tables are not judged as packs: si1, tk1a, tk1b, tk2a, tk2b, ru1, mcd11, mcd12, xy0, mcd14, mcd15, mcd16, mcd17, mcd18, sma, mcd19, fut20, swsh45sv, cel25c, swsh9tg, swsh10tg, swsh11tg, swsh12tg, swsh12pt5gg, me55c. Their cards stay in the catalogue; the shelf and the pack-battle picker do not offer them.

## Method

Each set is dealt 120 packs. A wrong card count, a rare-or-hit count other than one, a hit rate outside 3σ, a reverse or Basic Energy that should not be there, a card out of its slot or a wrong finish is an outlier. With 132 sets, about 0.4 three-sigma flags per sweep are expected from chance alone, so a flagged set is re-drawn at a larger N before it is believed.
